Typography and Text Styling

The Art of Typography in Web Design

Typography is one of the most important aspects of web design. It affects readability, user experience, and the overall aesthetic of a website. Good typography creates hierarchy, guides the reader's eye, and enhances the message.

The choice of font family can dramatically change the feel of your content. Serif fonts like Georgia convey tradition and reliability, while sans-serif fonts like Arial offer a modern, clean appearance.

Line height and letter spacing are crucial for readability. Proper spacing prevents text from feeling cramped and makes long passages easier to read. A line-height of 1.6 to 1.8 is generally considered optimal for body text.

Font weights allow you to create visual hierarchy without changing size. Headings typically use heavier weights, while body text uses regular or light weights for comfortable reading.
